Gliplog is a calm, private journal for people taking a GLP-1 medicine, whether a brand-name pen or tablet or a compounded vial from a pharmacy.

<b>What it does</b>
<ul>
<li>Log each dose in a couple of taps, with the injection site if you like, and see when the next one falls.</li>
<li>Log your weight in kilograms or pounds and follow the trend over time.</li>
<li>Add your height to track your BMI, with the WHO adult bands explained plainly.</li>
<li>See how your change so far sits beside the published average from the clinical trials of your medicine, each figure linked to its US prescribing label.</li>
<li>See an estimate of how much medicine is still in your body, drawn from the published half-life and peak time.</li>
<li>For compounded vials, enter the strength once and every dose also shows the units to draw on a U-100 syringe.</li>
<li>Note side effects, HbA1c and blood glucose, and keep progress photos that only you can see.</li>
<li>Set dose reminders, and export a PDF report to take to an appointment.</li>
</ul>

<b>Private by design</b>
<ul>
<li>No account, no server, no analytics, no ads.</li>
<li>The app requests no network permission. Your data never leaves your phone unless you save a backup file yourself.</li>
<li>Free and open source.</li>
</ul>

<b>Important</b>
Gliplog does arithmetic and shows published reference figures. It does not recommend doses and is not medical advice. Talk to your prescriber or pharmacist about your treatment.
