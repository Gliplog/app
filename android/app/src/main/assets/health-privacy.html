Gliplog and Health Connect

Sync is off until you turn it on in Gliplog, under Settings, Health apps.

What Gliplog reads and writes

  • Weight
  • Blood glucose
  • Blood pressure
  • Resting heart rate

You choose which of these sync, and whether they come in, go out, or both.

Where it goes

Readings move only between Gliplog and Health Connect on this phone. Gliplog has no account, no server and no network access, so it cannot send your data anywhere else.

Other apps you allow in Health Connect can read what Gliplog writes there.

Turning it off

Turn sync off in Gliplog, or remove Gliplog's access in Health Connect. Readings already synced stay where they are.
